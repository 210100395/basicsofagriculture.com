What is Agriculture?

Agriculture can be defined in several ways, but keys words like crop and animal , productin and other related science fields that describe agriculture should appear in the definition.

Agriculture is a practice of growing crops and rearing animals on land for people's use.

Types of agriculture?

  1. Crop production
  2. Animal production

Crop production

It is a broad aspect of agriculture in the world that deals with growing of various crops on the farm either edible or non-edible ones which act as raw materials to other agricultural products. Some of the crops grown in the world are, maize, sorghum, millet, rice, beans, onion and tomatoes.

Crop Production

Animal production

This focuses on keeping livestocks on the farm for different purposes, like, food, gives an access of raw materials for agricultural products. Some of the animals raised in the world are, chickens, goats, sheep, cattle and rabbits.
